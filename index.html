When an alarm goes off, the first few minutes decide what happens next, and too often guards arrive without knowing what they're walking into. Our security mission is to become a fully autonomous partner to security teams: first on the scene, and giving every responder eyes on the situation before they arrive.

It starts with the sites that matter most: estates, business parks, warehouses and critical infrastructure. When an alarm is triggered or the drone spots something out of place, it flies straight to the spot, streams live video to the control room and the responding team, and records everything as it happens.

The drone

Built to get there first.

Every Tambora drone is designed and built by us in South Africa, and flown by TamboraAI, the brain that lets it fly itself and understand what's happening on the ground. It doesn't wait for a guard to drive over. It's already in the air.

Always on

Patrols 24/7

Flies its site day and night. When its battery runs low, it lands on a rooftop dock that swaps the battery automatically and sends it straight back up.

Sees

Eyes that never blink

Watches fences, gates and open ground in the dark, with thermal vision. It knows what a normal night looks like, so anything out of place stands out straight away.

Responds

First to every alarm

When an alarm triggers, it's over the spot in moments, so the control room can see whether it's a real threat or a false alarm before anyone is sent.

Reports

Live to the people who need it

Streams a live feed to the control room and the responding team, so guards know exactly what they're walking into, and records everything as evidence.

Supervised

Safe by design

Flies itself, overseen by licensed remote pilots who can step in at any moment. Every flight follows South African aviation rules, and people not involved in an incident are blurred.

Learns

Smarter every flight

What one drone learns today, every drone knows tomorrow. Each patrol teaches TamboraAI more about the sites it protects.

Where we start

Eyes on every alarm.

We start by making sure no alarm goes unchecked. Our first security drones deploy in 2027, patrolling the sites they protect day and night and flying to every alarm the moment it's triggered.

Control rooms see the scene live before anyone is sent, so false alarms are cleared in seconds and real ones get the right response straight away. Guards arrive knowing how many people are there, where they went and what they're facing. Every flight also teaches TamboraAI more about the places it protects.

Where we're going

Safer streets, before anything happens.

Today, most security is reactive: something happens, an alarm goes off, and someone drives over to check. We're building towards a South Africa where help is already watching, and trouble is stopped before it starts.

First, connected networks of drones that cover whole neighbourhoods and business districts together, sharing what they see so a threat spotted in one place is tracked across the next.

Then, drones that do more than watch: lighting up a scene, speaking to people on the ground, and guiding responders straight to where they're needed, so every guard, every community and every business is better protected.

Every drone we fly today brings that future closer. Our goal is simple: everyone in South Africa should feel safe where they live and work.
